How It Works

Coaching That Fits Your Life, Not A Court Schedule

Same whole chain approach as in-person lessons. I look at your technique and the chain of legs, hips, core, and arm behind it, delivered async, wherever you are.

Step 1

Send Footage

Film a game or drill session on your phone, 1-2 times a week, and upload it through Crestline. No special equipment, no scheduling around a court.

Step 2

Get Reviewed

I break down the footage and send back feedback, written or live voice and video depending on your plan, on your technique and the chain of movement behind it.

Step 3

Drill The Fix

Every review comes with a custom drill plan built around exactly what I see in your footage, so you know exactly what to work on before the next review.

Everything happens through Crestline: footage submission, feedback, and your drill plans all live in one place, so you can see your progress from review to review.

Before You Sign Up

Questions

How does virtual pickleball coaching work?

It runs on the three-step loop above: you upload footage through Crestline, I break it down and send feedback in the format your plan includes, then you get a drill plan built around exactly what showed up in that footage. Most players send a game or drill session once or twice a week, so the review cycle actually keeps pace with how often you're playing.

Do I need to live near Florida to work with you?

No, that's the whole point of this page. In-person lessons and mobile chiropractic care only run in Volusia County, FL, but this program has clients well outside Florida since footage review doesn't care where you're standing when you film it. If you do happen to live nearby, in-person lessons are still worth a look too.

What's included in a Crestline coaching program?

The plan and pricing table above breaks down exactly what each tier includes. The short version: every plan gets footage review and a custom drill plan, Pro and Premium add live voice-and-video feedback with same-day priority turnaround, and Premium adds strength and nutrition guidance on top of that. Most players start on Standard and move up once they know how much feedback they actually use.

What if I don't have game footage yet?

That's fine. Any video from your phone works to start, even a casual drill or practice game. No special camera or setup needed. Once I see how you move and play, I'll tell you exactly what to film next to get the most out of your review.

Can I switch or cancel my plan later?

Yes. Crestline virtual coaching is a monthly subscription you manage directly in your Crestline account, so you can change plans or cancel anytime before your next billing date to match how much feedback you want.
