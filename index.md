### Questions

What makes a chiropractor-coach different from a regular pickleball coach?

Most coaches only see your technique. I look at the whole chain that produces the shot, your legs, hips, core, and arm, and how well they're working together. Most breakdowns come from that chain firing out of order. A physical limitation shows up sometimes too, and I'll catch that along the way. Either way you get the real cause instead of a guess.

How does virtual pickleball coaching work?

Through Crestline, you send me footage of your games or drills: 2 video reviews a month on Pro, 4 on Premium. I review it and send back voice-and-video feedback with live commentary (the free trial is a written diagnosis), plus a custom drill plan built around exactly what I see. No travel, no scheduling around a court.

Do I need to live near Florida to work with you?

No. My in-person lessons are limited to Volusia County, FL (my chiropractic rehab plan has a fully online option), but virtual coaching through Crestline works with players anywhere in the world. All I need is footage of your games or drills. The coaching works the same no matter where you play.

What's included in a Crestline coaching program?

Crestline starts with a free gameplay diagnosis. Monthly plans are $99 for 2 video reviews or $199 for 4, both with voice-and-video feedback and a custom drill plan; the $199 plan adds strength, rehab, and nutrition support.

What level of player do you work with?

Mostly serious recreational players in the 3.0 to 4.0 range who are ready to break through a plateau, plus higher-level players wanting a second set of eyes on their game. Put simply: 3.5 is the intermediate rating and 4.0+ is advanced, and I coach the intermediate player who wants to get there. Not sure where you fall or if it's a fit? Just reach out and ask.

Team lessons for league teams and regular groups of 4 to 8 players are $50 per player: an hour on court with your games filmed and coached, then a video breakdown of each pair's film afterward, shared with your notes in a private YouTube playlist and a Google Doc you keep. Private lessons ($85 an hour) and small-group lessons ($50 per person for 2 players, $40 per person for 3) are also available. Virtual coaching through Crestline starts with a free gameplay diagnosis, with monthly plans after that.

For in-person lessons, I ask for at least 12 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Last-minute no-shows affect other players' time too. Crestline virtual coaching is a monthly subscription you can pause or cancel anytime before your next billing date.
