Before You Book

Questions

Which option should I pick?

If you want the fastest improvement, go 1-on-1. A pack brings it down to as low as $65 a session. If you have a regular partner, a 2-player group gets you nearly the same attention for less. League teams should do Team Hybrid. If you're not local, go virtual. Still not sure? Text me and I'll point you to the right one.

What makes you different from other coaches?

I'm still competing at the 5.0 level, so what I teach is what I'm using on the court right now. I have 13+ years of racket sport experience. And I'm a rehab chiropractor and movement specialist, so I can look at the chain behind your swing (legs, hips, core, and arm) and catch what's really driving or limiting your shot. More on that in how I'm different from the average pickleball coach in Daytona Beach.

Can I take lessons if I'm dealing with pain or an injury?

Usually, yes. That's where my chiropractic background helps most. I'll work around what hurts, and a lot of the time we find that a mechanical issue in your stroke is feeding the pain. If something needs real treatment first, I'll tell you.

What does a typical lesson look like?

We start by figuring out where you're at and what we're focusing on that day. Then we go over the technique, introduce the skill, and drill it. I feed most reps by hand, so I can control pace and placement live. I also have a ball machine when we want a different kind of rep work. At the end, your 2 to 3 main focuses for the week go up in your portal.

What level of player do you work with?

Mostly 3.0 to 4.0: intermediate (3.5) players who want to reach advanced (4.0+) play. That's the serious recreational player who plays consistently and wants to keep improving. Not sure where you fall? Just ask.

Where do you teach?

Most lessons happen at Chipshot in Daytona Beach. It's indoor and air conditioned, and a court is always open. I also teach at public courts in New Smyrna Beach, Port Orange, Daytona Beach, and Ormond Beach during quieter hours. Tell me where you are and we'll pick the spot.

Do you bring the equipment?

Balls and a ball tube for pickup, always, plus a ball machine when we need it. You just show up with your paddle.

What's your cancellation policy?

I ask for at least 12 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. Life happens, I get it. A heads-up goes a long way.
