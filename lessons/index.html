Before You Book

Questions

What makes you different from other coaches?

A few things. I'm still competing at the 5.0 level, so what I teach is what I'm using right now on the court. I have 13+ years of racket sport experience, so the fundamentals are deep. And I'm a rehab chiropractor and movement specialist too, so I can look at the chain behind your swing, legs, hips, core, and arm, and catch what's really driving or limiting your shot. Most coaches only see half the picture.

What does a typical lesson look like?

We start by figuring out where you're at, what you want to work on, and what we're focusing on that day. Then we go over the technique, introduce the skill, and drill it. Most reps I feed by hand since I can control the pace and placement live. I've also got a ball machine for when we want a different kind of rep work, but we don't always need it. At the end you'll get your 2 to 3 main focuses for the week uploaded to your portal.

What level of player do you work with?

Mostly 3.0 to 4.0. In plain terms, intermediate (3.5) players who want to reach advanced (4.0+) play: the serious recreational player who plays consistently and wants to keep improving. If you're not sure where you fall, just reach out and ask.

Where do you teach?

Most lessons happen at Chipshot in Daytona Beach: it's indoor, air conditioned, and there's always a court, so no rainouts and no waiting. I also teach at public courts around Volusia County when they're not busy. The most popular is Pettis Park in New Smyrna Beach, and there are courts in Daytona Beach and Port Orange we can use during quieter hours. Tell me where you are and we'll pick the spot that works.

Do you bring the equipment?

Balls and a ball tube for pickup, always. I've also got a ball machine, but I'm good enough at feeding by hand that we don't always need it. You just show up ready to play with your paddle.

How do I track my notes and progress between lessons?

After every lesson your notes and drill assignments go into your own client portal. You'll always know what we covered, what to work on, and you can see your progress from week to week.

What's your cancellation policy?

I ask for at least 12 hours notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. Life happens, I get it. Last-minute no-shows affect everyone's time so a heads-up goes a long way.

How do I book?

Use the booking link on this page, DM me on Instagram, or send an email, whatever's easiest. Instagram DM is usually the fastest way to reach me.
