Before You Book

Questions

Why do you need footage before we can start?

Because without footage, I’m guessing. The whole program is built around what I actually see in your game — your patterns, your tendencies, your technical breakdowns. It’s also what makes your diagnostic call valuable: we’re working off real evidence, not assumptions.

How do I submit footage?

You’ll maintain an unlisted YouTube playlist and send me the link. Upload footage as you record it — games, drills, whatever you’ve got. I access it whenever I’m ready to review. No files to re-send, no back and forth.

What if I can’t get game footage right away?

Drill footage works too — solo or with a partner. We work with what you have and build from there.

What’s a realistic goal for 90 days?

That depends on where you’re starting, how consistently you’re playing and drilling, and how well you apply feedback. Most players who engage fully see measurable rating movement and a clear shift in how they’re playing. I won’t promise a specific DUPR jump because I don’t control how often you play — but the plan will be built to get you there.

What happens after 90 days?

We reassess. Some clients continue month-to-month as they push toward a new goal. Others feel ready to take things on their own and check back in periodically. No pressure either way.

How is this different from just taking more lessons?

Lessons fix what you can see in the moment. In a live lesson I’m tracking five things at once and I’ll catch two or three. On film, in slow motion, we catch the rest — and can tell if something’s a technical breakdown or a strategic decision that just looks the same in real time. Then it’s not “here’s some things to work on.” You get an actual drilling and strength program built off exactly what we found, with check-ins once or twice a week between calls.

What’s your cancellation policy?

Because this is a 90-day commitment with a capped cohort, I don’t offer refunds mid-program. If something genuinely unexpected comes up, reach out and we’ll figure it out. I just want people in who are committed and ready to do the work.
