### Why Most of This Happens Online

Fair question: I’m a chiropractor, so why isn’t this a stack of table visits?

- Table work is the smallest part of getting better. An adjustment can make you feel better for a few days. The plan is what changes how your body handles the court.

- The right exercises, progressed at the right time, done consistently. That’s what does the work. It takes programming and accountability, and neither one needs a table.

- Online, I’m with you every week. Not one visit and a printed sheet you lose by Thursday. Check-ins, form review, and a plan that changes as you do.

- It fits your life and your budget. Seeing someone in person two or three times a week for months adds up fast, in money and in drive time. This gives you the planning and oversight on your schedule, for a lot less.

Want regular hands-on work on top of the plan? That’s fine. I’ll point you to someone good and local, and your plan keeps running.

Before You Book

### Questions

How does the Hybrid Rehab Plan work?

It starts with one evaluation: in person if you’re local, by live video call if you’re remote. I test how you move, how strong you are, and how your body responds, then build your rehab plan from what I find and deliver it online. From there I manage it with you: weekly check-ins, video form review, and program progressions every 2 to 3 weeks. Local clients can also get one in-person session a month when it helps.

I don’t live in Florida. Can I still do this?

Yes, with one difference. I’m licensed as a chiropractor in Florida, so outside Florida the remote plan is movement coaching, not chiropractic care. I do a movement assessment by video, look at the movement and biomechanical factors that may be contributing to what you’re feeling, and build you a training plan, with the same weekly check-ins, form review, and progressions. I don’t diagnose or treat clients outside Florida. If something needs a diagnosis, I’ll tell you to see someone local first.

You’re a chiropractor. Why is most of this online?

Because the table work is the smallest part of getting better. An adjustment or soft tissue work can help you feel better for a few days. The plan, the progressions, and someone holding you to it are what change how your body holds up on court. None of that needs a table. Doing it online means I’m in touch with you every week, on your schedule, for a lot less than seeing someone in person several times a week.

Will I still get adjusted?

If you’re local and it’s useful, I can adjust you at your evaluation or a monthly in-person session. It isn’t the centerpiece of the plan. If you want regular hands-on work on top of the plan, I’ll point you to someone good and local, and your plan keeps running.

How long does it take?

The minimum is 3 months. Bigger or longer-standing problems usually need 6, and we decide that together at your evaluation. My goal is to get you to the outcome we set and able to keep it there on your own, not to keep you on a plan forever.

What does the first visit look like?

Your first visit is the evaluation. If you’re local, I come to your home with everything I need. If you’re remote, we do it on a live video call. We go through your history and what you want to get back to, then I test your movement, your strength, and how your body responds to different positions and loads. Plan on 60 to 90 minutes and wear something you can move in. Your plan follows, delivered online.

What’s included?

Your evaluation, a personalized rehab plan delivered online, weekly check-ins, video form review, and program progressions every 2 to 3 weeks. Local clients also get one in-person session a month when it helps. Most months, a quick call covers it.

What if I need more than one in-person session a month?

Most people don’t. They need a plan and someone holding them to it. If your case calls for more hands-on time, I’ll tell you, and we’ll work out what that looks like together.

How do I pay? Can I go month to month?

You have two options. Pay monthly, charged automatically to your card through Stripe each month for the length of your plan, or pay in full up front and take 10% off. Either way you’re committing to the full 3 or 6 months, because that’s the time it takes to build and progress a plan properly.

What does "done" look like?

You’re back to the goal we set at your evaluation, whether that’s playing again, playing without modifying everything, or competing, and you know how to keep it there yourself. My goal is to make you independent, not dependent on me.

What problems is this for?

Mostly musculoskeletal: back, neck, shoulder, hip, and knee problems, disc issues, sciatica, and sports injuries. If you play pickleball, I’m also looking at the movement chain behind whatever’s bothering you, not just the spot that hurts.

Do you take insurance, and do I need a referral?

There’s no insurance billing. This is cash-pay, though some HSA and FSA plans cover chiropractic care, so it’s worth checking with yours. No referral needed: chiropractors are primary care providers in Florida, so you can book directly.

What’s the cancellation policy for in-person visits?

I ask for at least 12 hours notice to cancel or reschedule. If I’m already on the way, or you’re not available when I arrive, the visit is forfeited or a cancellation fee applies. Life happens, but a heads-up protects both of our time.
