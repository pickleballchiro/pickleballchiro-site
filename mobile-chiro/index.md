### Questions

What does a mobile chiropractic visit look like?

First visit starts with a focused intake covering your history and goals, then we get to work the same visit. Every session includes a chiropractic adjustment, soft tissue work, and corrective exercise. Cupping and IASTM get added in as needed. First visits typically run 60 to 90 minutes. Wear comfortable clothes you can move in.

Do you offer multi-visit packages or an ongoing care plan?

Yes, but not as a fixed subscription. After your first visit I'll give you an honest read on what's going on clinically and how long I think it'll realistically take to resolve, then we build a plan around that together. Some people need one visit, some need several over a few months. It's arranged around your situation, not a flat monthly plan.

Do you come to my home, and do you take insurance?

Yes, I come to you: inside your home, the driveway, the garage, or the backyard. I bring everything, including a portable table. This is cash-pay care with no insurance billing, though some HSA and FSA plans cover chiropractic care, which is worth checking with your provider.

Do I need a referral for mobile chiropractic care?

No referral needed. Chiropractors are primary care providers in Florida, so you can book directly. I currently serve Daytona Beach, Ormond Beach, New Smyrna Beach, Port Orange, and Edgewater. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.

How often will I need mobile chiropractic visits?

It depends on what's going on. I'll give you an honest clinical recommendation after the first visit, and we work within your schedule from there. Nobody gets a generic frequency without me actually looking at your case first.

What conditions does mobile chiropractic care treat?

Mostly musculoskeletal: back pain, neck pain, shoulder and hip issues, disc problems, sciatica, sports injuries, and headaches. If you're a pickleball player, I'm also looking at the movement chain behind whatever's bothering you, not just the spot that hurts.

What's the cancellation policy for mobile visits?

I ask for at least 12 hours notice to cancel or reschedule. If I'm already in transit or arrive and you're not available, the session is forfeited or a cancellation fee applies. Life happens, but a heads-up protects both of our time.
